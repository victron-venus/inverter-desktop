# Code of conduct

We want participation in Inverter Desktop to be respectful and accessible to
people of every background and experience level. Use considerate language,
welcome questions, give constructive technical criticism, and respect privacy.
Disagreement about code is welcome; harassment and attacks on people are not.

Unacceptable behavior includes threats, discriminatory or sexual harassment,
personal attacks, sustained disruption, and publishing another person's private
information without permission. This policy covers project issues, pull requests,
reviews and other project spaces, and representation of the project in public.

## Reporting and enforcement

Send confidential conduct complaints to **[alvit.work@gmail.com](mailto:alvit.work@gmail.com)**,
the maintainer's existing public contact. Include links, relevant context and your
preferred way to be contacted. Do not publish private complaint details in issues.
Security vulnerability reports use [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

The maintainer reviews complaints, limits disclosure to what is necessary for
investigation, and explains the action taken where possible. Depending on severity
and repetition, action can include requesting a correction, removing content,
warning a participant, or temporarily or permanently restricting participation.
The affected person may request reconsideration by providing additional context
through the same private address.

This project currently has one human maintainer and **no independent moderator or
appeal body**. A complaint involving that maintainer cannot be promised independent
adjudication; platform policy violations may also be reported directly to GitHub.
Do not interpret this private channel as an emergency service or an anonymity
guarantee.
